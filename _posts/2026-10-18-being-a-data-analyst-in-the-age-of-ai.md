With all the news about big tech companies laying people off, I can’t help wondering if finding a job is going to get harder in the age of AI.

Especially for people like me, who are just starting to learn Python and trying to make a career change. 📊

But then again, seeing layoff headlines and assuming that AI is going to take away all our jobs might be a bit of a stretch.

AI can analyze data, write code, and generate reports. There’s no denying that.

So, the way Data Analysts work is definitely going to change.

For example, when looking at a company’s financial data, figuring out why the company is losing money or whether an investment is actually worth it takes more than just producing numbers.

Someone still needs to understand what those numbers mean and how to put that information to use.

At the same time, as AI tools get better, some tasks that once required a whole lot of work from a Data Analyst might be done by fewer people. That’s a possibility we can’t simply ignore, either.

So, where does that leave someone like me, who’s only just started learning Python?

For now, I think it’s a little too early to assume I’ve chosen the wrong path.

I guess I’ll just have to keep learning, see how things unfold, and figure it out along the way.

After all, I’ve already started this journey. Might as well keep running. 🤷‍♂️
