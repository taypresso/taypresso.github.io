Taypresso Myself! ☕️

Brewing a new career in Data Engineering

Hi there! I'm the person behind Taypresso!

I decided to change careers in my 40s. After years of working as a Marketing Engineer in the telecom sector, I’m now making a move into Data Engineering. That means starting over, more or less.

I’m bringing my previous work experience with me, even if I’m still figuring out how much of it will come in handy.

Why Taypresso? ☕️

I’ve written on Medium before, but this time, I wanted a space that could serve as both a personal journal and a portfolio. Somewhere to share my projects, document what I’m working on, and keep track of my progress.

So, GitHub Pages it is.

The name? Taypresso. Tay + Espresso. My name, mixed with coffee. A little silly, perhaps. The idea came to me while Sabrina Carpenter’s “Espresso” was still stuck in my head. And somehow, Taypresso just felt right.

What You'll Find Here

I love coffee, enjoy a good meme, and spend a good chunk of my free time working on projects here at Taypresso. This is my little corner of the internet where I build things, explore ideas, and document whatever catches my interest.

Life’s Joyful Glitches and Hectic Moments

Life doesn’t always go according to plan. Sometimes, things break. Sometimes, everything seems to hang, and there’s no obvious reason why. Sound familiar?

I’ve come to appreciate that not every glitch is a catastrophe. Some are frustrating in the moment but funny in hindsight. Others leave us with a lesson we wouldn’t have learned any other way.

I suppose that’s part of the fun of trying something new, whether it’s navigating a career change or figuring out why a piece of code refuses to work.

Nothing too fancy here. Just a guy in his 40s, a curious mind, and probably more coffee than necessary.

Thanks for stopping by!
